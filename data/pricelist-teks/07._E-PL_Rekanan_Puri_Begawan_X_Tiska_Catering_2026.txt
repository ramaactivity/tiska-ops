For over 35 years, TISKA Catering has set
the standard for culinary excellence,
creating unforgettable experiences that
elevate events of every scale. Our
philosophy combines traditional flavors
with modern creativity, ensuring each
dish is a masterpiece that delights the
senses.

Central to our journey is a remarkable
seven-year    partnership    with   Puri
Begawan Bogor. Known for its timeless
elegance and serene ambiance, Puri
Begawan provides the perfect backdrop
for extraordinary events. Together, we
offer bespoke solutions, seamlessly
blending our culinary artistry with the
venue’s    sophistication    to    craft
memorable celebrations.

This collaboration is more than a
partnership; it is a shared vision of
perfection. From enchanting weddings to
impactful corporate gatherings, Tiska
Catering and Puri Begawan transform
occasions into timeless legacies, ensuring
every moment is as exquisite as it is
unforgettable.
